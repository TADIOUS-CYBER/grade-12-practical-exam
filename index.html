Practical 1: Ethical and Legal Issues

Objective: Identify ethical and legal problems that occur when using information systems.

Activity 1: Identify the Problem

Choose the ethical issue and the correct action for each situation.

1. A student copies an internet assignment and submits it as their own.

2. A student shares a classmate's private photos without permission.

3. Someone accesses another person's email without permission.

4. A student installs pirated software on a school computer.

5. A person posts false information about a teacher on social media.
